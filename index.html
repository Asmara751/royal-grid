How to play

  1. Place exactly one queen in every row.
  2. Place exactly one queen in every column.
  3. Place exactly one queen in every color region.
  4. Queens can’t touch, not even diagonally.

Tap a cell once for × (a spot where no queen can go), twice for a queen, three times to clear it. Queens that break a rule get a red border.

Stars: 3 for a fast solve with no hints, 2 for a slower or one-hint solve, 1 for finishing.
